RecCue Support

RecCue · teleprompter for talking-head and beamsplitter shoots

How to reach us

Use App Support on the App Store product page. Feature ideas and bugs can also go in an App Store review. Until a public email is published, do not invent a contact address in the repo or review notes.

What the app does

Paste or import scripts (txt / rtf / docx), organise them in folders, scroll at a steady pace with mid-play tap-to-jump, adjust speed, type size, and side margins, and use mirror plus landscape for beamsplitter filming. Optional iCloud Documents sync for your script library. No account required.

Purchases

RecCue is a paid App Store download (one-time purchase)—not a subscription, and with no extra in-app purchases. Terms are Apple’s standard EULA. After switching devices, re-download with the same Apple ID.
