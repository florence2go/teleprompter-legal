RecCue Privacy Policy

Version: 16 September 2026. Covers the App Store listing RecCue (Simplified Chinese name 提词Pro).

What we do not collect

RecCue does not use ads, third-party analytics, or tracking, and it does not require an account. Script content is not uploaded to a developer-operated server. We do not sell or share that data.

How data is stored

Scripts you paste, import, or edit, plus folders and playback preferences (speed, font size, mirror, appearance, interface language), stay on this device. If iCloud is on for your Apple ID and this app’s iCloud Documents container is available, your script library can sync into your personal iCloud Documents container (iCloud.com.florence.Teleprompter). That container belongs to your Apple ID; the developer cannot browse it. If several devices write the same record, the last successful system write wins.

File import

The app reads a .txt, .rtf, or .docx file only when you pick it from Files, and it parses the text on device. Original files are not uploaded to developer servers.

Purchases

RecCue is a paid App Store download (one-time purchase). There is no subscription and no in-app purchase. Apple handles payment and receipts; we never see your payment card details.

Deletion

You can delete scripts and folders in the app. iCloud copies can be removed through Settings → [your name] → iCloud and related document controls. Uninstalling clears on-device data; whether iCloud documents remain depends on your iCloud settings.

Children

RecCue does not knowingly collect personal data from children and does not offer public chat, stranger interaction, or targeted ads.

Contact

For privacy help, use App Support on the App Store product page. See Support.
